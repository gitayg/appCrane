# AppCrane Commercial License

AppCrane is free and open source under the [GNU AGPL v3.0](LICENSE). You can
use, modify, and self-host it at no cost.

The AGPL is a **network copyleft** license: if you run a **modified** version of
AppCrane as a service that others interact with over a network, you must make
your complete corresponding source available to those users under the AGPL.

## When you may want a commercial license

A separate commercial license removes the AGPL's copyleft obligations. Consider
it if you want to:

- run a **modified** AppCrane as a hosted / SaaS product **without** publishing
  your modifications, or
- **embed** AppCrane inside a proprietary, closed-source product, or
- otherwise distribute AppCrane under terms incompatible with the AGPL.

Using **unmodified** AppCrane, or self-hosting it internally while complying with
the AGPL, does **not** require a commercial license.
